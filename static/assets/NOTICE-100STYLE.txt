Real-Time Style Modelling of Human Locomotion via Feature-Wise
Transformations and Local Motion Phases.
https://doi.org/10.1145/3522618
Original dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/8127870

TIMELESS adapts samples from Neutral_FW.bvh and Neutral_FR.bvh.
The samples are cropped to one cycle, resampled, phase-aligned to the
existing lower-body animation and adapted to the character's skeleton.
